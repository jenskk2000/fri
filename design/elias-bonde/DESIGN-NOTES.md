# FRI concepts inspired by eliasbonde.dk

Reference inspected live in the Codex Browser on 22 September 2026: https://eliasbonde.dk/

## Observed design principles

- Strong dark/light section contrast: a photographic, near-black hero followed by spacious white and cool-gray editorial sections.
- Heavy geometric headings. Live computed styles identify Montserrat at weight 800; body and controls use Inter, with regular body text and semibold buttons.
- Core ink is `#0B0D12`. Inspected accent values include mint `#4ADE9B` and blue `#2F4BFF`; supporting body text is `#3F4756`.
- Short, tightly led headlines with selected words highlighted in green. Small uppercase, letterspaced section labels establish hierarchy.
- Candid personal photography establishes a human presence. Dark overlays preserve hero-text legibility; other photos sit in large rounded containers.
- Pill-shaped CTAs, clear filled-primary/outlined-secondary hierarchy, subtle borders and restrained shadows.
- A handwritten green wordmark provides an informal counterpoint to the otherwise precise typography.
- Spacious section rhythm, readable text widths, restrained metadata chips and straightforward Danish language.

These observations describe the inspected homepage; no claim is made about exact font licensing or uninspected pages. Palette values above are computed styles; mockup proportions and the cool-gray background are design interpretations.

## Translation into FRI

Retain FRI's purpose and free/account-free scope. Make the running DKK estimate the main home-screen element, with the craving-support action easy to reach. Keep the streak secondary. Borrow typography, contrast, photography treatment and shape language, while creating an original FRI identity. Do not reproduce Elias's portrait, name, personal claims, media logos or endorsement.

1. **Dark editorial home:** photographic atmosphere, large white savings figure, mint highlights.
2. **Light editorial home:** airy layout and white savings card, with a small everyday-life photo.
3. **Mixed home:** white canvas, a dominant dark savings card and a simple daily check-in.
4. **Onboarding welcome:** personal photography and one clear first step, with no account or subscription.

All figures are illustrative: 50 DKK per 20-pouch tin, 10 pouches/day, 15 days gives an estimated 375 DKK. Static mockups do not demonstrate live counters or widget behavior. The native app implementation is unchanged by this exploration.
